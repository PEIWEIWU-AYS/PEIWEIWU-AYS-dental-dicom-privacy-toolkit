# Code of Conduct | 行为准则

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be useful, careful, and welcoming for people working on
dental imaging privacy, open-source healthcare tooling, and research workflows.

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good intent, and
keep patient safety and privacy at the center of technical discussion.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Keep discussions focused on synthetic data and reproducible evidence.
- Explain tradeoffs clearly when discussing privacy or security.
- Credit prior work and reference tools honestly.
- Avoid overstating clinical, legal, regulatory, or security claims.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Sharing real patient data or private clinical materials.
- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory comments.
- Publishing private credentials, URLs, tokens, or institutional information.
- Pressuring others to provide real clinical files for debugging.
- Making unsupported claims of clinical approval or compliance certification.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and issues that violate this code
of conduct or the repository safety boundary. Repeated or severe violations may
lead to blocking from the project.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, code review,
and public project spaces.
